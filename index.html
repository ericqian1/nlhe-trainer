Preflop Trainer $1/$2 · 9-handed · $200 stacks

Action

    Pot & bet

    Pot = what was in the middle before this round of betting. Facing a raise? Put your own bet in Your bet already in and the raise total in Villain raises to. Toggle bb / $ to enter and read amounts in either (1bb = $2).

    Villain ranges

    Every villain acts from these charts. They are loaded from data/ranges.csv — edit that file to change how a profile plays. The Pro charts are also what your decisions are graded against. Mixed cells mean either action is accepted (villains pick one at random).

    Villain mix

    Pick how many of each player type sit at the table (9 seats). Each hand you take over a random seat and play its cards in place of that villain; everyone else stays in their seat for the session.

    Seats

    Change any seat directly, or shuffle who sits where. Changes apply from the next hand.
